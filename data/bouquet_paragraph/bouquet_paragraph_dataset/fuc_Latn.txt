Hakke teddungal e natal hoore mum ina reena e nder Espaañ, haa teeŋti e nder Doosɗe leydi ndii e nder Bayyinaango winndannde jojjanɗe hakkeeji aade. Ko ɗum waɗi ndimaagu haalde ina foti faamreede e nder gonka, sibu sariya ina reeni ɓiɗɓe leydi e tooñanngeeji e kappeeji baawɗi bonnude natal mum en. So ɗum waɗii, ñaawoore warhoore ina toɗɗii ko adii fof, ko e neɗɗo kaaloowo ɗum ɗe. Kono, ko ɗum huunde himmunde no feewi, so tawii ɗeen haauluji peewnaa koy enternet, gardiiɗo hello ngoo ina waawi kadi naŋngeede to bannge sariya so tawii binnduɗo ɗeen haaluuji waawaa anndeede. Yanti heen, coyngal e kuuge pawaaɗe ɗe ɓuri toowde so tawi tooñanngeeji e kappeeji ina layi e nder laylayti renndo, sibu bonanndeeji ɗi ngaddata ɗii ina hiisee e ko ɓuri mawnude. Waɗde ndeentoɗee e haaluuji e lowre mon walla e kronik enternet mon.
Miin ko mi kawjoo e sahaa kala. Kono jaɓde ɗum e yeeso yimɓe ina kadi saɗi e am. Won miijo duumiingo renndo ngo wonde yimɓe hawjuɓe ɓe ina njogii ŋakkeende, huunde nde foti ko feewneede, walla ina foti yaafnaade sahaa kala.
Feewnitde jaŋde ina jeyaa e caɗeele mawɗe ɗe ganndu-ɗaa eɗen poti darnaande ɗum e leydi ndii. Ƴellitaare karallaagal e mbayliigaaji renndo-demokarafik ngaddi caɗeele kese ɗe njuɓɓudi men hannde ndii mbaawaa heen hay huunde. Ɓeydagol almudɓe e kala ekkol e sarɗiiji kuuɓtodinɗi ɗi jannginooɓe kuutortoo golle mum en, in mbaɗi batte to bannge moƴƴingol jaŋde. Ina haani nde jaɓɓotoo ɗen ɗee ɗo naamnde fof, so tawii en njiɗii yontaaji kesi keɓa jaŋde toownde e kadi njokken kadi e yahrude yeeso e nder renndo ngo.
Yimɓe ɓe ina jogii heen miijo janngude. Filoosofuuji gerek en teskiima ɗum gila ko adii, mbinndi heen ko heewi. Ko ɓuri heewde e men ina njiɗi anndude no geɗe ɗee ngoniri e kadi hol ko waɗi ɗe ngonti noon. Te kadi, en ndokkii jaɓde won e goongaaji ɗi mbaɗata en e njinndaangu walla ɗi en njiɗaa.
Jam hiiri. Miɗo welaa kadi miɗo weltii e yeewtidde e mon fof laawol gootol ngam yiɗande on jamma weltaare e kawral. No hitaande kala e weetndooga nowel, miɗo yiɗi yeewtidde e mon miijooji am e ngonka leñol men e caɗeele paaɗi e men e nder leydi men. Ina jeyaa e naamnde nde njiɗ-mi yeewtude heɓde koɗki, batte mbayliigaaji weeyo e njuɓɓudi imikrasioŋ, sabaabuuji mum en ina keewi ina ceerti. Miɗo yiɗi waɗde ɗum e nuunɗal e goonga.
Min kollitii ballal wonande yimɓe ɓe ilam yani e lewru marse ɓennundu nduu. Ngal ballal ina fawii e ustude njoɓɗeele faggudu jowitiiɗe e ŋakkeende nguura ɓurndi sokleede. Naamnii ɗii ina foti waɗeede e ordinateer hakkunde 6 e 30 abriil tawa ko ndeen tan. Doggol kominaaji jowitiiɗi e noddaango e winndannde ina tawee e hello adannde nde.
Madam Hooreejo Suudu Sarɗiiji, Banndiraaɓe Rewɓe e Worɓe Depiteeji, on njaaraama. Miɗo ɗoo ɗaɓɓude woote hoolaare e ndeeɗoo suudu ngam toɗɗaade gardiiɗo jaagorɗe. Njeñtudi woote ɗee ina laaɓti, ina hollita ballal banndiraaɓe men e jokkude eɓɓaande politik nde puɗɗi-ɗen duuɓi 4 jooni. Woote ɗe ɓiɗɓe leydi ndii kollitii e dow wellitaare e nder laabi sariya, ko golle demokaraasi yeru. Miɗo sakka on noon, Banndiraaɓe Rewɓe e Worɓe, eɓɓoore nde njiɗ-mi huutoraade so mi heɓii ballal keewngal e Suudu nduu.
