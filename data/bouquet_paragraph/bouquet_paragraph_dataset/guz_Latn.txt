Ase ebinto bigwonchoru gotumeka nainde bibekire gopokea obochafu bwa nyomba bori botabeku mokebe ime na gotugutu para. “Obochabu bwa nyomba” bomanyekanete buna obochafu korua ase ebinto bigokoreka ase chinka. Ensemo eye ebwate, amo nebinto binde, amabuta yokorosia endagera atumekire,ebinto biokoragerera, ebinto bie ‘stima, ebirogo,chimattress, ebinto bia nyomba, gokomenta no ‘bochabu ao korua ase ebinto bikorosigu.
Ogwancheru kwomonto gosika na gotwara ebicha yomonto nkorendire ime ya Spain, korendire ne richiko na ime ya Universal Declaration of Human Rights. Erio rende, obosibore bwo ‘gokwana goika boigwekane ase eke, ekiagera richiko ndirendete abanto korua amaramo nechingwana chirasari erieta riabo. Onye aya gagotoka, oria okwanete nabwenerete koirerwa egetambokero kierichiko. Korende, naya nayeng’encho mono, onye amang’ana aya akwaniru ase omotandao, oria otenenerete omotandao nairerwe egetambokero onye oria okwanete takonyoru. Komenya na aywo, egesusuro kere ekenene onye amang’ana aya akwaniru ase omotandao, aseng’echo ogosari kwaye kore igoro. Kabe maiso, ase igo, nenchinkwana ogoteba emetandao ime.
Botambe nabeire masomberera. Korende goancherana ase abanto nakong’u ase nde. Ntwe abanto tore masomberera, tobwate oboamate, ntobwate oboremaru, egento togochenchia gore gosaba amabera chingaki chionsi.
Goonchoreri amasomo ase ense eye nomochando omonene tobwate buna ense. Amaonchoreri gochia ase emetandao na edemokrafia-yo ‘mochiea yaretire emechando emenge chisistem chiato nchikonyara. Komentekana kwa abana ase ekerasi ekemo na buna abaorokia bagokora amang’ana abo nabwate obosegeta obonene gochia ase obosomi. Gokwaneranamang’ana aya nabe ayeng’encho gochia ase ebisaku gotwara chisemi nchingiya naintwe kogenderera buya buna ekenyoro.
Buna mwanyabanto ntobwate erang’o yo ‘koegera. Abasomi abagiriki mbamanyete aya nainde bakarika aso obogare amang’ana aya. Abange baito batageta komanya naki ebinto bire nainde naki bitaraba eng’encho eyio. Nainde, twangire goancherana ekeene kene nkeri gotoisaransera buya gose ntorigogeancha.
